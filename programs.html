Trades Training & Community Programs

Hands-on training in six
high-demand trades.

Real projects. Real certification. Real jobs. Our immersive program prepares students for immediate employment while rebuilding Detroit — three days a week, five hours a day.

Core trades

Choose your craft

Master framing to finish work through supervised, real-world renovation projects.

Electrical

Master circuits, wiring, and safety standards for residential systems.

Plumbing

Learn pipefitting, drainage, fixtures, and full system installation.

Carpentry

Develop framing, finishing, and custom woodwork through live builds.

HVAC

Train in heating, ventilation, and air-conditioning systems.

Masonry

Work with brick, block, and stone to build durable structures.

Welding

Gain expertise in welding techniques and metal fabrication.

The program

Built around how people actually learn a trade

  • Trade training & certification — industry-recognized credentials across six trades.
  • Professional mentorship — each student paired 1:1 with an experienced pro.
  • Hands-on renovation — restore real properties under supervision.
  • Community engagement — improve housing and rebuild neighborhood pride.
  • Job placement — move into apprenticeships and full-time roles with partners.

Schedule

3 days a week, 5 hours a day (10 AM – 3 PM). Flexible enough to fit your life.

Certification

Graduate with recognized credentials that employers value from day one.

Placement

Direct pipeline into apprenticeships and full-time jobs with partner companies.
