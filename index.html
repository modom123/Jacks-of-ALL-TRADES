The Process

How Our Program Works

A structured five-phase journey from classroom to career, with mentorship and community impact at every step.

01
Trade Training & Certification
Comprehensive instruction across six high-demand trades with industry-recognized certification
02
Professional Mentorship
Each student matched with an experienced professional for guidance and ongoing support
03
Hands-On Renovation
Students restore real properties under supervision, gaining authentic practical experience
04
Community Engagement
Projects improve housing, beautify lots, and rebuild neighborhood pride across Detroit
05
Job Placement
Graduates move into apprenticeships and full-time positions with partner companies

Community Revitalization

Training Through Real Construction

We directly address urban blight across Detroit by training students through actual renovation projects that transform the city.

🤝
Partner & Identify
Work with city government, land banks, and housing authorities to identify vacant and dilapidated homes across Detroit neighborhoods.
🏚️
Acquire Properties
Acquire or lease selected properties as dedicated training sites where students develop real-world skills under professional supervision.
🔨
Restore & Train
Engage students in all phases of renovation: demolition, repair, drywall, roofing, plumbing, landscaping, and electrical work.
🏡
Distribute Homes
Sell, rent, or donate completed homes to low-income families, or use them as mentoring housing for program participants.
📈
Reinvest & Expand
Reinvest all proceeds into additional property restoration projects, expanding our reach and impact across the city.
