# Road to $100,000 — Campaign Plan

**Goal:** fund the $100,000 renovation of a Detroit home.
**Anchor event:** 50/50 drawing — Dec 28, 2026, Ford Field (Monday Night Football halftime).
**Site:** joatamp.org · **Give:** Zeffy · **Track:** Command Center → Marketing.

## The funding mix (why not raffle alone)
The 50/50 splits every dollar 50/50, so the raffle alone would need **$200K** in sales to net $100K. Blend instead:

| Source | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Direct donations (Zeffy) | $35–45K | 100% to mission, no split — most efficient |
| 50/50 raffle (net to reno) | $20–30K | Reach + excitement; feeds the funnel |
| Corporate / trade sponsors | $20–30K | 2–4 partners @ $5–10K (suppliers, contractors, banks) |
| Foundation / grant | $15–25K | Detroit housing + workforce funders |

## Weekly rhythm until Dec 28
- **Email** the list every 1–2 weeks: reno progress + live pot + "buy tickets / give."
- **Social** 3–4×/week: before/after, student stories, "pot is now $X," countdown.
- **Local media** pitch: Bridge Detroit, Detroit Free Press, WDIV/WXYZ, BLAC Detroit.
- **Partners & churches** share the raffle link to their lists.
- **Peer-to-peer:** each board member/volunteer sells 20 tickets to their circle.

## Channels
- **Facebook** — best for donations; join Detroit neighborhood groups.
- **Instagram** — before/after reels, countdown stickers.
- **LinkedIn** — corporate sponsors + grant credibility.
- **Email** — the #1 online fundraising channel; send even to a small list.

## Grants to pursue
Gilbert Family Foundation, Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Foundation, Kresge Foundation,
Detroit Land Bank partnerships, City/State workforce-development funds.

## Paid (optional, only after organic is running)
$5–10/day Facebook/Instagram boost to metro Detroit + retarget site visitors.

## Milestones
- **Launch week:** publish story + photos, email #1, first 5 social posts, ask board to share.
- **Mid-campaign:** first sponsor secured, media pitch sent, progress email.
- **Final 2 weeks:** countdown push, "last chance before Ford Field," daily social.
- **Draw day:** livestream the drawing, thank donors, publish the winner + total raised.
