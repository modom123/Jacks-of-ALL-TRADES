# Donate video — text & social captions

**The one rule:** a video file itself is never tappable. When you text or post a
video, tapping it just plays it. People reach the donation page through the
**link in your caption/message** (or a link sticker / bio link). So always send
the video **with** one of the links below.

## The link to use

- **Works right now:** `https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/ticketing/jacks-of-all-trades-community-development`
- **Shorter / nicer (after the site update deploys):** `joatamp.org/donate`
  — same destination, easier to type/remember. Use once it's live.

## 📸 Instagram
- **Stories/Reels:** post the video, then add the **Link sticker** → paste the link.
  Viewers **tap the sticker** to open the donation page. Add text like
  “Tap to donate 👆”.
- **Feed post:** captions aren’t tappable on IG, so say **“Link in bio.”** Put the
  link in your bio (or a Linktree). Business/nonprofit accounts can also add a
  **Donate** action button on the profile.

> Detroit has thousands of vacant homes. We train young Detroiters in 6 skilled
> trades and rebuild those homes — by hand. 🔨
> 100% of your gift funds the mission.
> 👉 Tap the link (sticker/bio) to donate.
> #Detroit #SkilledTrades #Nonprofit #GiveBack #JacksOfAllTrades #DetroitStrong

## 🎵 TikTok
- The caption link isn’t clickable on TikTok. Two options:
  1. **Link in bio** (available on Business accounts / at eligibility) → paste the
     link there and say “link in bio.”
  2. Tell viewers to go to **joatamp.org/donate** (easy to type) or scan the QR.

> We turn vacant Detroit houses into homes — and young people into skilled tradespeople.
> Donate: link in bio (or joatamp.org/donate) 🔨🏠
> #Detroit #Trades #Nonprofit #fyp #DetroitTok

## Pro tips
- **Put the ask ON the video too:** the QR code + “Donate at joatamp.org/donate”
  shown in the last few seconds means people can act even if they never look at
  the caption. (You already have the QR — I can burn it + a caption onto the
  video for you if you want a version with it baked in.)
- **Pin the comment:** on every platform, pin a comment with the link so it’s the
  first thing people see.
- **Same link everywhere** keeps it memorable and lets you track it.
