```
Jacks of All Trades Apprenticeship and Mentoring Program (JOATAMP) is a Detroit-rooted nonprofit that combines workforce development and community revitalization in one self-sustaining model.

We train disadvantaged youth in carpentry, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, masonry, and drywall using abandoned and dilapidated homes as live job sites. Students earn industry-recognized credentials, get 1:1 mentorship from experienced tradesmen, and move directly into apprenticeships and full-time roles with partner employers.

Each restored home adds safe, affordable housing for Section 8 residents, low-income families, and people experiencing homelessness, and it brings new tax and utility revenue to the city.

Partners include the Detroit Land Bank Authority, Detroit City Council, Section 8 housing providers, and local contractors.

We are looking for employer partners, contractors, material donors, and funders. Let's build together.
```

## 6. Raffle promotion note

Organic posts about the 50/50 raffle are fine. **Paid ads** for raffles count as gambling on Meta, TikTok, and Google, and they need prior written authorization or aren't allowed at all. Don't boost raffle posts. Boost mission, program, and before/after posts instead. Include "18+, Michigan residents only" when you post about the raffle.
