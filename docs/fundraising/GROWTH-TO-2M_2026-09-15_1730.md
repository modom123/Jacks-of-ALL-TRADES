# Growing to a $2,000,000 annual budget

**Reality first:** going from $0 to $2M/year is normally a **3–5 year** climb, and it is won by **diversifying** — no healthy nonprofit leans on one source. The number itself isn't the hard part; **organizational readiness** (clean books, outcome data, audit, board, staff capacity) is what unlocks the big grants. Build that in parallel.

| **Total** | **$2.0M** | No single source over ~40%. |

## Phased roadmap

**Year 1 — Foundation (~$250K–$400K).** Land 2–3 anchor grants (one federal, one foundation), formalize 2–3 corporate partners, run the raffle, and build a recurring-donor base. Get books, EIN/501(c)(3) docs, and outcome tracking audit-ready. *This is the credibility year.*

**Year 2 — Scale (~$800K–$1.2M).** Win a multi-year federal award, stand up **earned revenue** (home sales/rent + trainee contract work), add major gifts and an annual event. Hire against restricted grant budgets, not ahead of them.

**Year 3+ — $2M, diversified.** Recurring multi-year government contracts, a corporate sponsor portfolio, a self-sustaining earned-revenue engine, and a 3–6 month operating reserve.

## What unlocks the big money (do these now — most are free)

- **Confirm the legal entity + EIN** on file matches your IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter exactly (applicant name **Jacks of All Trades**, EIN **41-2680557**). Grants are rejected over mismatches.
- **Register** on [SAM.gov](https://sam.gov) (free UEI number — required for all federal grants) and [Grants.gov](https://grants.gov).
- **Track outcomes from day one**: # trained, # certified, # placed in jobs, wages, # homes rehabbed, $ of neighborhood value returned. Funders buy outcomes, not activities.
- **Audit / financial review**: many funders over ~$100K require one. Start with a bookkeeper + reviewed financials.
- **Board**: recruit members with grant, corporate, and finance networks.
