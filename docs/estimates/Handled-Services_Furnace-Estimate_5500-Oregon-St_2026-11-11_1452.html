Project Summary

Furnish and install new high-efficiency gas furnaces for both flats at 5500 Oregon Street. The building is a two-family flat of 3,200 sq ft total: upstairs and downstairs flats of 1,600 sq ft each. Each flat has 4 bedrooms, 2 baths, a kitchen, a dining room and a living room. The building has a full basement, where both furnaces will be installed.

Why two furnaces: Each dwelling unit in a two-family building needs its own heating system. Supply and return air may not be shared between units under the Michigan Residential/Mechanical Code. Separate systems also give each flat its own thermostat and its own gas bill. One shared furnace cannot legally serve both flats.

Design basis (preliminary)

  • Detroit outdoor heating design temp: about 6°F; indoor 70°F
  • Estimated heat loss: lower flat about 45–55k BTU/h; upper flat (roof & attic exposure) about 60–72k BTU/h
  • Final sizing confirmed by Manual J (Item A1) before equipment is ordered. If the size changes, the price is adjusted at cost.

Equipment selected

  • Lower flat: 60,000 BTU input, 96% AFUE (about 57,600 BTU output)
  • Upper flat: 80,000 BTU input, 96% AFUE (about 76,800 BTU output)
  • Sealed-combustion PVC direct vent, ECM blower, MERV 11 filtration, Wi-Fi thermostats

  • Structural, framing or chimney masonry repair (other than the liner allowance)
  • Air conditioning, humidification and full duct replacement unless accepted as options above
  • Work outside the scope listed above. Any change is done only by signed change order.
