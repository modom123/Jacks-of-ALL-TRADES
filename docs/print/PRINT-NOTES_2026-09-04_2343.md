## Specs
- **Size:** US Letter, 11 in x 8.5 in, **landscape** (2 pages = 2 sides)
- **Fold:** Standard tri-fold / letter fold, 3 equal panels (~3.67 in each)
- **Page 1 = OUTSIDE** (left -> right: back cover | mission panel | front cover)
- **Page 2 = INSIDE** (left -> right: "Your Ticket" | before/after reveals | ticket pricing)
- **Color:** Full color, CMYK for offset printing
- **Paper:** 100# gloss/matte text or 80# cover recommended
- **Print full color to the edge.** No crop/bleed marks are included; if the shop
  needs bleed, ask them to add 0.125 in bleed and scale to fit.

of page 1) shows on the back. The right inside panel (Ticket Pricing) is the
tuck-in flap. Confirm panel order with your printer before the run.
