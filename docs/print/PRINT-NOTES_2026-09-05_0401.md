### IMPORTANT - review the tax wording before printing
The flyer intentionally says donations "may be tax-deductible to the extent
allowed by law" rather than "100% tax-deductible." Reasons:
- Deductibility requires the org to be a qualified 501(c)(3). Confirm status/EIN.
- Gifts of goods (windows, appliances, materials) are generally deductible at
  fair market value, subject to IRS limits - not a guaranteed flat 100%.
- Donated **labor/services** (e.g., install time) are **not** tax-deductible.
- Raffle **ticket purchases** are **not** tax-deductible (separate from donations).
If your CPA confirms specifics, the wording can be tightened accordingly.
