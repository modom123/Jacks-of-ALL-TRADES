## Background color - print guidance
The cream/tan flood is a design choice, not the cheapest/safest option:
- **White background** = least ink, most consistent color, no risk of banding/mottling
  on digital presses, lowest cost. Navy + gold still carry the brand. **Best for
  budget/short-run digital printing.**
- **Tan/cream (current)** = warmer, more premium look. Fine on **offset or a good
  digital press on coated stock**, but large light flat tints can occasionally show
  streaking on cheaper digital output, and edge-to-edge flood uses more ink.
A white-background variant can be produced on request.
